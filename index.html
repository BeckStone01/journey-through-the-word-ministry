Welcome Home

A ministry for people who want more than a Sunday moment.

Faith grows when the Word becomes part of everyday life. This ministry exists to teach biblical truth, strengthen families, comfort the hurting, and help believers become active disciples of Jesus Christ.

Whether you are returning to faith, looking for prayer, or searching for a place to serve, there is room for you on the journey.

Bible-centered teaching that helps people understand Scripture and apply it with wisdom.

02

Serve With Love

Outreach shaped by compassion, practical care, and the call to be the hands and feet of Christ.
